GTA 6 completion trackers

GTA 6 is out — and these are the real lists, built from the game's own 100% checklist and confirmed launch-week data: 2,100+ items across 16 trackers. Tick items off as you play. Progress saves in this browser.

How the trackers work

Each tracker is a checklist built for completionists. Use the tabs to switch between categories — missions, vehicles, collectibles and locations — and the search box to find a specific entry without scrolling. Tick items off as you earn, drive, find or visit them, and the progress bar shows how much of that category you have covered. Your progress saves automatically in this browser on this device, so you can close the page mid-session and pick up exactly where you left off. There is no account and nothing to sign up for; the trade-off is that progress lives here, on this device, rather than following you around.

What is in each tracker

100% Checklist is the flagship: every requirement from the game's own checklist (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion), in its three groups — Story & Tasks, Exploration & Activities, and Discovery. Story Missions tracks all 93 main missions across the prologue and five chapters. Collectibles covers the hunts: 480 cards in 18 sets, 125 cocaine flamingos, 33 underwater discoveries, magazines and book swaps, meditation spots, basketball hoops and WAiNK landmark scans. Stunts holds all 397 jumps, bridges, knife flights and base jumps. Jobs & Tasks tracks gang operations, bank jobs, stick ups, smash 'n' grabs, vaults and optional tasks, and Activities covers range golds, drone trials, races, hunting quests and challenges. The catalogues — 277 vehicles to scan, 255 animals to study, 66 standard weapons to hold — round out the Discovery group, and Locations tracks the six regions of Leonida; pair it with our Leonida guide for a full tour of the state.

How these lists are built

Every count and requirement here comes from the game itself — its built-in 100% checklist and stats screens — cross-checked against published launch-week guides. Where a name or a location is not yet confirmed (individual mission names, vehicle and animal names), the tracker says so plainly and tracks by number until the real name is verified; names and locations are being added across launch week as they are confirmed. That is the same rule the rest of ViceHub runs on — our verification policy explains it — and it means that when you tick something off here, it corresponds to something that actually exists in the game.

New to Leonida? Start with the launch checklist, then work the 100% Checklist tab above — it is the shortest path through everything on this page.
