GTA 6 completion trackers

Tick items off as you play. Progress saves in this browser. Full lists go up after launch on November 19, 2026.

How the trackers work

Each tracker is a checklist built for completionists. Use the tabs to switch between categories — missions, vehicles, collectibles and locations — and the search box to find a specific entry without scrolling. Tick items off as you earn, drive, find or visit them, and the progress bar shows how much of that category you have covered. Your progress saves automatically in this browser on this device, so you can close the page mid-session and pick up exactly where you left off. There is no account and nothing to sign up for; the trade-off is that progress lives here, on this device, rather than following you around.

What is in each tracker

Missions will list the story's missions in order once they are known, so you can see your progress through Lucia and Jason's story at a glance and spot anything optional you skipped. Vehicles covers the cars, boats and aircraft you have owned or driven — in a game where the Leonida Keys make boats as important as cars, this list will be longer and more varied than GTA 5's. Collectibles is for the scattered hunts open-world games hide across their maps; it exists because finding 95 percent of something from memory is misery. Locations tracks the regions and landmarks you have visited, from Vice City to Mount Kalaga — pair it with our Leonida guide if you are planning a full tour of the state.

Why the lists are short until November 19

Honesty about timing: the full, verified lists cannot exist yet. Rockstar has not published mission counts, vehicle lists or collectible totals, and any site showing you a complete GTA 6 checklist today invented it. So the trackers launch with what is genuinely known from official material, and the complete lists go up after release, built from the real game and checked as they grow. That is the same rule the rest of ViceHub runs on — our verification policy explains it — and it means that when you tick something off here, it corresponds to something that actually exists in the game.

Until then, the most useful prep is the boring kind: finish the launch checklist, lock in your fan zone predictions while the future is still uncertain, and get ready to start ticking boxes on November 19.
