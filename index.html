What is GTA 6?

Grand Theft Auto VI is Rockstar Games' next open-world game, and the follow-up to GTA 5, which launched back in 2013. That thirteen-year gap is a big part of why the hype around this release is so intense: an entire generation of players has grown up waiting for the series to move on from Los Santos. GTA 6 takes the series back to Vice City, but not as a remake or a nostalgia trip. Vice City is one city inside Leonida, a whole fictional state based on Florida, with its own beaches, swamps, island chains, farming towns and a national park.

The story follows two protagonists, Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, a couple pulled into crime together across the state. Rockstar has shown them robbing, driving, boating and running from the law across trailers that cut between quiet character moments and full-scale chaos. You can read their confirmed backstories on our characters page, and explore every region Rockstar has shown so far in our guide to Leonida.

Your launch plan, in order

Launch is close enough that the useful thing now is a plan, not more rumours. Here is the order we recommend, based on the dates Rockstar has confirmed:

  • NowDecide which edition you want and where you are buying it. Reported US prices are $79.99 for Standard and $99.99 for Ultimate. Our preorder comparison runs through what each edition includes and the digital-versus-physical trade-offs.
  • This weekFree up storage. Rockstar has not announced the final file size, and estimates are large, so clearing space early avoids a launch-week scramble. Our file size guide explains the estimates and how to make room on PS5 and Xbox.
  • November 12, 2026Digital preloads open. If you bought digitally, start the download as soon as it appears and leave your console in rest mode so it finishes well before launch day.

None of this requires buying anything extra. It is simply the difference between pressing play at unlock and spending launch night watching a progress bar.

Why Leonida is a bigger deal than it sounds

Every GTA game is really about its place, and Leonida is the biggest place Rockstar has ever built. Rockstar says the map is about twice the size of GTA 5's Los Santos and Blaine County, but the more interesting change is variety. GTA 5 had one city and a lot of desert and mountains. Leonida, as shown in the trailers, mixes a dense neon city, working ports, island keys joined by causeways, alligator swamps, sugar country and forested highlands. Each region has its own look, its own local characters and, going by the trailers, its own kinds of trouble.

That variety is exactly why we built a full Leonida guide and a map size breakdown: one explains what each region is and what Rockstar has actually shown of it, the other separates the official size claims from fan estimates, which are interesting but much less reliable. When the game launches, our locations tracker will let you tick off every region as you reach it.

Confirmed, reported, rumoured: how to read this site

GTA 6 attracts more fake leaks than almost any game in history, so ViceHub labels everything. Official means Rockstar said it directly, in a trailer, on the Newswire or on the game's official page. Reported means trusted outlets have published it but Rockstar has not confirmed it, which includes the US prices on this page. Anything else you read elsewhere — exact Online dates, PC dates, file sizes presented as fact — should be treated as speculation until Rockstar says otherwise, because right now Rockstar has not.
