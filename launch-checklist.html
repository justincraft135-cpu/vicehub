GTA 6 launch-day checklist

Updated October 31, 2026

Do these things before November 19 so you can start playing the minute the game unlocks.

  • Free up storage. Final file size is not confirmed. Clear plenty of space on your console's internal drive or a fast expansion drive.
  • Turn on automatic downloads. Preloads open November 12. Leave your console in rest mode so it downloads overnight.
  • Check your subscription. If online features arrive later, you will likely need PlayStation Plus or Xbox Game Pass Core.
  • Pick your edition. Compare what each edition includes before you buy. Physical copies may still need a day-one download.
  • Update your console. Install system updates the day before launch to avoid waiting on launch night.
  • Clear your schedule. Servers and stores tend to be busy on launch night, so give yourself extra time.

The plan by week

  • Now to November 8The decisions. Pick your edition and store, preorder if you are going to, and sort storage. These are the only steps that involve money or big downloads of a different kind — deciding which games to delete — so they are the ones not to leave to launch week.
  • November 9 to 11The console prep. Install any system software updates, update your controller firmware if your platform offers it, check your TV is set to its game mode with HDR calibrated in the console settings, and make sure your PlayStation or Xbox account can sign in without a forgotten password standing in the way.
  • November 12Preload day for digital copies. Start the download as soon as it appears in your library, then leave the console in rest mode with an internet connection and automatic downloads enabled. On a slow connection this can take most of a day — see the download-time table — which is exactly why you start on the 12th and not the 18th.
  • November 18Check the preload shows as complete, and watch for a day-one patch downloading on top of it. Charge controllers and headsets. Decide your spoiler plan: if you care about story surprises, consider muting GTA terms on social media, because earlier time zones will be playing before you.
  • November 19Launch day. Unlock times have not been officially announced yet; check the release date page for the latest. When it unlocks, resist the urge to rush — the opening hours of a GTA story are the ones fans will talk about for years.

Settings worth five minutes on day one

Every big launch has the same handful of settings that quietly shape your first hours, and GTA 6 will be no different in kind, whatever its specific menu names turn out to be. Before you start the story, it is worth checking: whether the game offers a performance or fidelity mode, and picking based on your screen rather than habit; subtitle size and speaker labels, which matter in a game this dialogue-heavy; camera and aim sensitivity, plus aim-assist strength, before muscle memory sets in; and HDR brightness, calibrated in the console's own settings first so the game's own slider starts from a sane place. None of this needs doing before launch — it is a five-minute investment at the start of your first session that pays off for a hundred hours.

Your account and subscription, checked early

The checklist above mentions subscriptions for a reason. Story mode is the launch event, but when online features arrive you will very likely need PlayStation Plus or Xbox Game Pass Core to use them, based on how both platforms handle online play generally. There is no need to subscribe now for a mode with no announced date — our GTA 6 Online page tracks what is actually known — but do make sure the account you preordered on is the account you intend to play on, and that you can sign into it. Launch-night password resets are a rite of passage nobody enjoys.

If something goes wrong on launch night

Big launches strain everything, so a little triage knowledge helps. If the game will not unlock, fully restart the console before panicking — store licences often refresh on reboot. If a download stalls, pause and resume it, or restart the console in rest mode, rather than deleting and starting over; you almost never need to re-download the whole game. If servers or stores are slow, that is normal for the first hours of a release this size and it resolves itself. And if your physical copy has not arrived, check the retailer's tracking before assuming the worst — release-day deliveries for major launches often land late in the day. Whatever happens, do not buy a second copy from a marked-up reseller in a moment of frustration; every one of these problems is measured in hours, not days.
