GTA 6 characters: who is in the game?

Updated October 31, 2026

GTA 6 has two playable main characters, Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, a couple drawn into crime together in the state of Leonida. Lucia is the series' first female lead in the modern era.

Lucia Caminos

Lucia grew up with her mother's dream of a better life, first in Liberty City and then in Leonida. She spent time in prison, and now she is out and determined to take what she wants instead of waiting for it.

Jason Duval

Jason had a rough childhood, then joined the Army to get away from it. After leaving, he ended up in the Leonida Keys working for local drug runners. Meeting Lucia could be the best or worst thing that ever happened to him.

Other confirmed characters

  • Cal Hampton

    Jason's friend, a conspiracy theorist who listens in on police radio.

  • Boobie Ike

    A Vice City local with a strip club, a studio and other businesses.

  • Dre'Quan Priest

    A music manager who runs a record label with Boobie.

  • Real Dimez

    A rap duo signed to Dre'Quan and Boobie's label.

  • Raul Bautista

    An experienced bank robber who looks for new partners.

  • Brian Heder

    A drug runner in the Keys who gives Jason work and a place to stay.

Can you switch between Lucia and Jason?

Rockstar has shown both characters but has not fully explained how switching works. GTA 5 let you swap between three characters, so many fans expect something similar.

Lucia and Jason: what their story seems to be about

Rockstar's trailers and character profiles sketch a clear shape for the story, even though the plot itself is still secret. Lucia is the driver of the pair. She grew up between Liberty City and Leonida, did time in prison, and has come out resolved to stop waiting for permission to live well. Jason's arc runs the other way: the Army was his escape from a rough upbringing, and drifting into work for drug runners in the Leonida Keys was supposed to be a stopgap, not a life. Lucia walking into that life is the event the whole story hangs on — Rockstar's own framing is that meeting her could be the best or worst thing that ever happened to him.

That push and pull is what makes the setup more interesting than a simple crime spree. One partner wants more, faster. The other knows exactly what the life costs. Whether the story tests their loyalty, their luck or both is speculation at this point Speculation — Rockstar has not described the plot beyond the profiles — but the tension is built into the confirmed material, and it is why so much of the trailer footage cuts between the two of them rather than following a single lead.

How the supporting cast fits together

The confirmed supporting characters are not random; they map onto the different worlds Lucia and Jason move through. Brian Heder is Jason's entry point into the Keys' drug trade, the man who gives him work and a roof. Cal Hampton is Jason's friend on the paranoid fringe, the kind of character GTA uses to comment on the world while also feeding the plot information — in his case, by listening to police radio. Over in Vice City, Boobie Ike's businesses — a strip club, a studio, more — put him at the centre of the city's nightlife economy, and Dre'Quan Priest's record label, run with Boobie, connects that world to Real Dimez, the rap duo signed to the label. Raul Bautista, the veteran bank robber looking for new partners, is the most obvious bridge between the supporting cast and the kind of scores the trailers show Lucia and Jason pulling.

What we do not know yet is how much of the story each of them carries, who can be trusted, and who is set up to betray whom. GTA stories traditionally braid these circles together until they collide, but any specific claim about a character's role in the plot that you see online right now is guesswork. We will update this page as Rockstar profiles more of the cast.

Confirmed vs speculation

Confirmed
Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval are the two playable protagonists. Their backstories above, and the supporting cast list, come from Rockstar's official site and profiles.
Confirmed
Lucia is the first female protagonist to lead a modern GTA game, and the story is set in Leonida with the pair operating as a couple.
Not confirmed
How character switching works. GTA 5 allowed instant swaps between its three leads, but Rockstar has not explained GTA 6's system, and it may work differently when the two leads are together for much of the story.
Not confirmed
Voice actors, character ages, mission counts per character, and whether either protagonist can die or be left behind by the story. None of this has been announced.
Not confirmed
Returning characters from GTA 5 or the original Vice City. Rumours circulate constantly; Rockstar has confirmed none.

That split is the honest state of knowledge less than three weeks before launch. The confirmed list is short because Rockstar guards its stories carefully — which is also why the launch-day experience should still hold surprises.
