GTA 6 unlock time by time zone

GTA 6 launches on Thursday, November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. If you bought it digitally, the game does not open at the same clock time everywhere — it unlocks at one moment worldwide, and what that moment looks like on your clock depends on your time zone. Digital preloads open a week earlier, on November 12, 2026, so the download can be finished and waiting well before unlock.

Find your city in the table below to see the expected local unlock time — and read the status note first, because Rockstar has not confirmed the exact hour yet.

Official status

Expected Not confirmed by Rockstar

Rockstar has not announced an official unlock time for GTA 6 yet. The launch date — Thursday, November 19, 2026 — and the November 12 preload date are official. The exact hour is not.

The times in the table below follow the pattern used by most big digital launches: the game unlocks at midnight Eastern on launch day, and every other time zone is worked out from that moment. Treat them as the best current expectation, not a promise. We will update this page the moment Rockstar confirms the official unlock time.

When can I preload?

Digital preloads open on Thursday, November 12, 2026 — a full week before launch — for anyone who has pre-ordered the digital edition on PS5 or Xbox Series X|S. Start the download as soon as it appears and leave your console in rest mode so it finishes in good time.

Preloading only downloads the game files. A finished preload does not let you play early: the game stays locked until the unlock time in the table above, wherever you are.

What if I bought the disc?

Physical copies play as soon as you have the disc in your console — there is no time-zone unlock to wait for. If your copy arrives on launch day, you can put it in and play.

Expect a day-one update to download and install before you start, so build that waiting time into your launch-day plans and make sure your console is connected and updated the night before.

Does Xbox unlock at the same time?

Expected

For the digital edition, yes — the Xbox Series X|S version is expected to unlock at the same moment as PS5: midnight Eastern on November 19, 2026, and at the matching local times in the table above. Neither Rockstar nor Xbox has confirmed platform-specific unlock times yet, so if either store announces something different, this page will be updated.

One caution: changing your console's region settings to chase an earlier unlock can break preorders and store access. It is not worth the risk for a few hours.
