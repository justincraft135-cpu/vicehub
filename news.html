The road to launch: what each announcement actually changed

December 2023 — the reveal

The first trailer did three things at once: it confirmed Vice City and the wider state of Leonida as the setting, introduced Lucia as a lead character, and put a 2025 window on the game. It also set the pattern for everything since — Rockstar says very little, very rarely, and lets the footage do the work. Almost everything solid we know about the game's world still traces back to that trailer and the two that followed.

May 2025 — trailer 2 and the first delay, days apart

May 2025 was the strangest month of the whole cycle. On May 2, Rockstar moved the release from fall 2025 to May 26, 2026. Four days later it released the second trailer, a much deeper look at Jason and Lucia, captured on PS5. The pairing was deliberate in effect if not in intent: the delay stung, but the footage showed a game visibly further along, and attention shifted from the date to the world. That trailer remains the best single source for the characters' story, which is why our characters page leans on it.

November 2025 — the second delay

The move from May 26, 2026 to November 19, 2026, announced November 6, 2025, reset plans across the whole industry, not just for fans. It also, paradoxically, increased confidence in the final date: publishers avoid announcing a specific day, rather than a season, unless they intend to hit it. The full delay history is laid out on our release date page.

June 2026 — preorders open

Preorders opening on June 25, 2026 turned GTA 6 from an event into a product: editions, prices and store listings. The reported US prices — $79.99 Standard, $99.99 Ultimate — are labelled Reported on this site because they come from retail coverage rather than a Rockstar price announcement. Our preorder comparison tracks what each edition includes and where to buy.

October 2026 — the launch trailer locks it in

The October 15, 2026 launch trailer is the most recent and most consequential entry: it confirmed November 19 on screen and added the detail planners actually needed, digital preloads from November 12. A game does not get a preload date unless it is finished in every way that matters. That is why the remaining news between now and launch is expected to be practical — unlock times, file size, launch-week details — rather than anything that changes the plan.

How we check GTA 6 news

The GTA 6 rumour economy is enormous, and most of it is wrong. Screenshots get faked, "insiders" invent dates, and old reports get recycled as new. Our rule for this timeline is strict: an item appears here only if Rockstar published it, or if several established outlets have reported it independently. Every entry carries a source link so you can read the original yourself, and the Official and Reported tags tell you at a glance how strong the sourcing is.

What we leave out matters as much as what we include. We do not list leaks, even plausible ones, because a fan site that repeats a leak as news cannot be trusted when it counts. We do not repeat single-source rumours about PC dates, Online dates or file sizes as facts; where those topics are genuinely useful, they live on their own pages — GTA 6 Online, the FAQ — clearly labelled as reports or speculation. And when we get something wrong, the fix goes on the page with an updated date, not a quiet edit. Our full policy is on the About page.
