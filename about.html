About ViceHub

ViceHub is an independent fan site that collects GTA 6 news, release info and guides in one easy place. We are fans, not Rockstar.

All facts are checked against public reports and Rockstar's own announcements, and each page shows when it was last updated.

What ViceHub is for

ViceHub exists because GTA 6 information is scattered across three years of trailers, announcements, delays and reporting, and buried under an avalanche of rumours. We collect the verified core of it in one place, organised the way a player actually needs it in the run-up to launch: when the game comes out, what it costs, how much storage it needs, who is in it, where it is set, and what to do before November 19, 2026. Alongside the facts we run fan tools that are ours alone — polls, launch predictions, community rankings and completion trackers — so the site is somewhere to participate in the countdown, not just read about it.

Our editorial and source-verification policy

This is the policy every page on this site is written under, and it is the main reason the site exists in its current form.

1. Rockstar first

If Rockstar has said something — in a trailer, a Newswire post, an official page or a platform listing — that is the top of the evidence chain and the claim is labelled Official. Where Rockstar has said nothing, we say so plainly rather than filling the gap.

2. Reporting needs corroboration

Claims from the press are labelled Reported, carry a source link, and generally appear here only when several established outlets agree. Prices are the clearest example: the $79.99 and $99.99 US prices are widely reported and consistent across retailers, but Rockstar has not issued its own price announcement, so they stay labelled as reported everywhere on this site, including the pages that help you spend money.

3. Speculation is labelled as speculation

Analysis, fan estimates and our own reasoning are useful — the fan mapping projects estimating Leonida's size are genuinely impressive — but they are never dressed up as fact. If a number comes from measuring trailer footage, the page says so. If a date for PC or GTA 6 Online comes from a report or a fan prediction, the page says that too, and our fan predictions are presented as a game fans play, not as information.

4. No leaks as news

We do not build pages on leaked material, datamined claims or anonymous social-media "insiders". Apart from the harm leaks do to the people making the game, they are wrong often enough that a site repeating them cannot be trusted about anything else.

5. Corrections and updates

Every guide page carries an "Updated" date, and pages are revised when facts change — when official unlock times are announced, when store listings publish the real file size, when Rockstar says anything new about Online. If you spot an error, email us at the address below with a source and we will check it against primary material and fix the page if you are right. We would rather be corrected by a reader than be confidently wrong at scale.

How the site is funded

ViceHub is free to read. Some outbound links, clearly placed in boxes labelled as affiliate content, are affiliate links — mainly to Amazon — and if you buy through one we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate, ViceHub earns from qualifying purchases. The site also carries advertising. Neither affects what we write: affiliate products are the ordinary things a GTA 6 player might need, like storage drives, and our labels policy applies to prices and claims in those boxes exactly as it does everywhere else. Our privacy policy explains what data the site handles.

Who runs ViceHub

ViceHub is made by fans, in our spare time, because we have been waiting since 2013 like everyone else. We are not Rockstar, we have no inside sources, and we do not pretend otherwise — the site's whole method is to work only from public material and to show its working. If that approach sounds slow compared with the rumour mill, it is. It is also why, when this site tells you a date or a price, you can plan around it.
