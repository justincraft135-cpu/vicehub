    How the fan zone works

    The fan zone is where ViceHub stops telling you things and starts asking. There are three things to do here, and they work together. The polls are quick either-or questions about the choices every player is already arguing about — who you will play as first, how you are buying the game, which edition, and what you will do first when Leonida opens up. Voting takes one tap, the results update with the community's picks, and you can change your mind any time before launch; plenty of people do as new trailers land.

    The predictions are the longer game. You set a Metacritic score, a first-week sales figure, and dates for the two things Rockstar has pointedly not dated: GTA 6 Online and the PC version. Lock them in now, while they are genuinely uncertain. After launch we will score the predictions against reality — the review scores, the reported sales, and the eventual announcements — which is what turns a guess into a result. The ranking asks you to order six features by hype, and aggregates everyone's order into the community ranking: a running snapshot of what fans actually care about, which has a habit of changing after every trailer.

    Why we run it this way

    Two principles. First, your picks are yours: they are stored so you can return, revisit and revise them, and the prediction questions deliberately cover things nobody knows yet — that is what makes them predictions rather than a quiz. Second, the fan zone never pretends to be news. When the prediction form offers "Early 2027" as an option for GTA 6 Online, that is a choice in a fan game, not a reported date; our GTA 6 Online page is where the actual state of knowledge lives, carefully separated into official, reported and speculation. Conflating the two is how rumours are born, and this site exists partly to not do that.

    New here? Start with the polls — they take under a minute — then make your predictions while the launch is still 19 days of arguing away. When you have played the game, the completion trackers are where the fan zone hands over to the long game of finishing everything Leonida has to offer.
