GTA 6 FAQ

Updated October 31, 2026

When is GTA 6 coming out?

November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. See the release date page.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not at launch. No PC date has been announced.

Is GTA 6 on PS4 or Xbox One?

No. It is only announced for current-generation consoles.

Will GTA 6 have online multiplayer at launch?

Reports say online will not be available on day one. Rockstar has not shared an online release date.

Where is GTA 6 set?

In the fictional state of Leonida, which is based on Florida and includes Vice City.

Who are the main characters?

Lucia and Jason, a criminal duo.

When can I preload GTA 6?

Digital preloads open November 12, 2026.

More questions, answered

How much does GTA 6 cost?

Reported US prices are $79.99 for the Standard edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate edition. Rockstar has not announced pricing directly, so treat these as reported rather than official, and confirm at checkout. Full comparison on the preorder page.

What is the difference between the Standard and Ultimate editions?

Both include the full base game. The Ultimate edition is reported to add extra vehicles, weapons and story content. Both are reported to include the Vintage Vice City Pack as a preorder bonus. If you mainly want the story and the world, Standard is the complete game.

How big is the GTA 6 download?

Rockstar has not announced the file size. Estimates cluster around 150 to 200 GB, possibly more with a day-one patch, so we recommend clearing about 250 GB. The size should appear on the store listings when preloads open. See the file size guide for download-time estimates.

What time does GTA 6 unlock on November 19?

Rockstar has not announced unlock times. Big console launches often unlock at midnight local time for digital copies, but that is a pattern, not a promise for this game. We will update the release date page as soon as official times exist. Be wary of exact times quoted anywhere else right now; they are guesses.

How big is the map?

Rockstar says Leonida is about twice the size of GTA 5's map. Fan mapping projects estimate more, roughly 2.4 to 2.7 times, but those are measurements from trailer footage. Details and every region are on the map size page and in our Leonida guide.

Is GTA Online shutting down when GTA 6 launches?

Nothing has been announced about GTA 5's Online mode ending. Rockstar has also not announced a launch date for GTA 6's own online mode; reports say it will not be there on day one. Everything known and rumoured is separated out on our GTA 6 Online page.

Can I buy a physical copy?

Yes, major retailers list physical editions for PS5 and Xbox, alongside digital. One caution from the preorder listings: some physical copies may be a download code in a box rather than a disc, so check the specific listing if a disc matters to you.

Can I preload if I buy physical?

No. Preloading from November 12 is for digital copies bought from the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store or as digital codes. A physical copy installs when it arrives, and will likely still need a download before it can play.

Will my GTA 5 progress or character transfer to GTA 6?

Rockstar has not announced any transfer of characters, money or progress from GTA 5 or GTA Online to GTA 6. Assume you start fresh unless Rockstar says otherwise.

Why was GTA 6 delayed?

The release moved twice: from a 2025 window to May 26, 2026, then to November 19, 2026. Rockstar's announcements gave the new dates without a detailed public breakdown of reasons, and we do not invent one. The full timeline is on the release date page.

Do I need an internet connection to play?

You will need internet to download or install the game and any day-one patch, which applies to almost every modern release. Rockstar has not published specific always-online requirements for the story mode, so we will not guess; if that matters to you, check the store listing closer to launch.

What should I do before launch day?

Three things cover most of it: free up storage, set up preloading from November 12, and install console updates ahead of time. Our launch-day checklist walks through all of it in order.
