GTA 6 file size: how much space do you need?

Updated October 31, 2026

Rockstar has not announced the official file size yet. Most estimates put GTA 6 at around 150 to 200 GB, and a day-one patch could add more. To be safe, plan on clearing about 250 GB before preloads open on November 12, 2026.

What we know

Official size
Not announced. It should show on the PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store listings once preloads open.
Estimates
About 150 to 200 GB for the base game, possibly 200 to 250 GB with the day-one patch.
Preload date
November 12, 2026 (digital copies)
Physical copies
Expect a large install and a download even with a disc or code in a box.

Is the 676 GB Xbox screenshot real?

No. A screenshot showing GTA 6 at about 677 GB spread on social media, but several sites have called it fake. Neither Rockstar nor Microsoft has posted a size.

Will it fit on my console?

A PS5 has roughly 667 GB of usable space and an Xbox Series X roughly 802 GB, so GTA 6 will fit on either, but you may need to delete a few big games first. The Xbox Series S has much less space (about 364 GB usable), so Series S owners will almost certainly need to clear room or add storage.

How to make room

  • Delete games you finished. Your saves stay in the cloud or on the console, so you can reinstall later.
  • Move older games to a USB drive. PS5 and Xbox can store last-gen games on cheap external drives.
  • Add an internal SSD. On PS5, an M.2 NVMe SSD with a heatsink works. On Xbox, use an official Seagate or WD expansion card.

Need more space? A 2TB drive gives you room for GTA 6 and the rest of your library.

How long will the download take?

File size only hurts if your connection is slow, so here is the maths that actually matters. The table uses the middle of the current estimate range, a 200 GB download, and assumes your connection runs at full speed the whole time, which real connections rarely do. Treat these as best-case times and add a buffer.

Two caveats. First, launch weeks slow everything down: platform servers under load can halve real speeds, which is precisely why preloads open a full week early on November 12. Second, the day-one patch is a separate download on top of the preload for many big launches, and its size is unknown. The safest routine for anyone on a slower connection is simple: preload the moment it opens, keep the console in rest mode with automatic downloads on, and check on November 18 that everything, patch included, shows as complete.

Storage maths for each console

Working with the 250 GB safety target from the top of this page: a standard PS5, with roughly 667 GB usable, or an Xbox Series X, with roughly 802 GB usable, will hold GTA 6 comfortably alongside a normal library, but if yours is nearly full of other large games you are still looking at deleting two or three of them. The Xbox Series S is the tight squeeze. With about 364 GB usable, a 200 to 250 GB install would leave room for almost nothing else, so Series S owners should decide now which games move to an external drive or get deleted, rather than discovering the problem during preload week.

One more trap: both consoles need working room beyond the game's listed size to unpack and install downloads, and updates over the game's life will grow the total. Clearing 250 GB is a floor, not a luxury. If you are buying storage, buy it before November if you can — expansion drives for both consoles reliably sell out or rise in price around a launch this size, and the affiliate box above lists the right categories for each system.
