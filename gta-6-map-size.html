How big is the GTA 6 map?

Updated October 31, 2026

Rockstar says Leonida is about twice the size of GTA 5's map and its biggest world ever. It has not given an exact size in square miles.

Official vs estimated

Official
About 2 times GTA 5, according to Rockstar's Aaron Garbut in a Game Informer preview
Fan estimates
Roughly 2.4 to 2.7 times GTA 5, based on community mapping projects
GTA 5 for reference
Around 49 square miles (about 75 to 80 square km)

Fan estimates come from people measuring trailer footage, so treat them as rough. The official "about twice" is the number to trust until the game is out.

Regions of Leonida

Leonida is Rockstar's take on Florida. These areas have been shown in trailers and on Rockstar's site:

  • Vice City

    The neon city based on Miami, with beaches, nightlife and the port.

  • Leonida Keys

    A chain of islands to the south, like the Florida Keys.

  • Grassrivers

    Swamps and wetlands inspired by the Everglades, full of wildlife.

  • Port Gellhorn

    A run-down beach town on the coast.

  • Ambrosia

    A farming and industrial area with a big sugar refinery.

  • Mount Kalaga National Park

    Forests, trails and outdoor activities in the north.

The regions, in more depth

Vice City is the anchor: Rockstar's version of Miami, all neon, beaches, art-deco streets and a working port. It is the region shown most in the trailers and the one returning players will compare with the 2002 original. Expect the density here — traffic, crowds, enterable businesses, nightlife — rather than raw land area to be what feels next-generation.

The Leonida Keys stretch south in a chain of islands joined by causeways, mirroring the Florida Keys. This is Jason's home turf in the story and Brian Heder's smuggling country. Water is the point of the Keys: boats, bridges and long drives over open sea will make this region feel unlike anything in GTA 5.

Grassrivers is the swamp: wetlands, airboats, alligators and small settlements on stilts, inspired by the Everglades. Regions like this usually hide the map's stranger side content, and the trailers lean into its wildlife and weather. Port Gellhorn, by contrast, is a faded coastal town — the trailers suggest somewhere past its best years, which in GTA terms usually means interesting characters and cheap property.

Ambrosia is the agricultural and industrial interior, built around a big sugar refinery, and Mount Kalaga National Park covers the forested north, with trails and outdoor activities. Together they answer a question fans asked after GTA 5: what fills the map besides city and wilderness? Here, the answer is working country — farms, industry and small towns with their own economies. Size is not the same as value

It is worth being a little sceptical of the size race, including of the numbers on this page. A map twice the size of GTA 5's only matters if the extra space is filled with things worth doing. GTA 5's map, for reference, was roughly 49 square miles of land and sea, and even it had stretches players mostly drove through. The reason Leonida is encouraging is not the multiplier but the mix: six distinct regions with different terrain, weather and wildlife, plus a city denser than Los Santos, suggests Rockstar spent the extra space on variety rather than emptiness.

How fan estimates are made, and why they wobble

Community mapping projects work by triangulating trailer shots: identify the same landmark in two frames, estimate distances from known objects like cars and road widths, and build a scale map piece by piece. It is impressive work, and their 2.4 to 2.7 times estimates may land close to the truth. But every step involves assumptions — about lens distortion, about which locations are truly the same place — which is why different projects produce different numbers and why we present the official "about twice" as the anchor figure. The only measurement that will settle it is the game itself, on November 19.
